Orogoo Job Portal — Web UI/UX Case Study

Responsive candidate dashboards and shift recruitment user flows for short-term jobs, connecting job-seekers with verified gigs through minimal friction and rapid booking.

CLIENT / PRODUCT Orogoo Enterprise
MY ROLE UI/UX & Web Designer
TIMELINE 3 Months (2023 - 2024)
TOOLS USED Figma, Angular, Bootstrap, CSS3
Visit Live Website
Verified Client Case Study
CASE STUDY FRAMEWORK

PROBLEM → PROCESS → OUTCOME

01
THE CHALLENGE & PROBLEM

High Friction in Discovery & Application Flow

Users faced cluttered job listings, confusing navigation filters, and multi-step registration hurdles that resulted in severe drop-off rates on mobile devices.

  • Dense, unstructured candidate filters causing user drop-off.
  • Slow mobile responsiveness and excessive checkout/apply friction.
  • Lack of instant visual trust indicators and verified badges.
02
THE DESIGN PROCESS & SOLUTION

User-Centric Architecture & Prototyping

Mapped end-to-end user journeys, validated interactive Figma prototypes through usability tests, and established a modular design system in both Figma and production code.

  • Wireframed 3-step streamlined discovery-to-action flow.
  • Built reusable component libraries (Angular, Bootstrap & Tailwind).
  • Synchronized design tokens with engineers for zero handoff loss.
03
THE MEASURABLE OUTCOME

Accelerated Conversion & Seamless Delivery

Delivered tangible business metrics including faster user onboarding, dramatic reduction in bounce rates, and high-fidelity front-end implementation.

  • 40% reduction in user onboarding & task completion time.
  • Zero visual inconsistencies between Figma specs and shipped code.
  • Positive client feedback and seamless production rollout.
