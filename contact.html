FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common queries regarding project timelines, workflows, deliverables, and communication.

What is your typical project turnaround time?

Small landing pages or UI kits take 1 to 2 weeks, while full web application designs and frontend builds take 3 to 5 weeks depending on scope complexity.

What design & development tools do you use?

For design, I rely on Figma, Adobe XD, Photoshop, and Illustrator. For development, I build with Angular, HTML5/CSS3, Tailwind CSS, and Bootstrap.

Do you provide developer-ready handoff files?

Yes! I provide organized Figma libraries with design tokens, component variants, spacing scales, and responsive code snippets ready for engineering integration.

Are you open to remote global opportunities?

Absolutely. I work seamlessly with international clients and remote teams across various time zones via Slack, Zoom, Jira, and Teams.
